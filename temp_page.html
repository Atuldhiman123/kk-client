How your zodiac element defines you

In astrology, the four elements are Fire, Earth, Air, and Water and they play a significant role in defining the characteristics and traits associated with each zodiac sign.

Fire Symbolises passion, energy, and enthusiasm. Fire signs are often seen as dynamic, confident, and action-oriented.

Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)

Representing stability, practicality, and reliability, Earth signs are grounded and dependable, with a strong sense of responsibility.

Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)

Air symbolises intellect, communication, and social connection. These signs are curious, adaptable, and good communicators.

Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)

Water signs are known for their emotional and empathic traits. Water signs are sensitive, nurturing, and deeply in touch with their feelings.

Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)
